# Permissions

Permission = Resource + Action + Scope.

## Actions
View, Create, Edit, Delete, Assign, Export, Approve, Refund, Send, Publish, Manage.

## Scopes
Own, Assigned, Team, Branch, Workspace.

## Default role templates
Owner, Admin, Marketing Manager, Marketer, Sales Manager, Sales Agent, Logistics Manager, Delivery Agent, Inventory Manager, Finance/Accountant, Support Agent, Analyst.

Role names are templates; authorization must evaluate permissions rather than hard-coded role checks.
