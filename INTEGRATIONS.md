# Integrations

## Adapter categories
MessagingProvider, PaymentProvider, AdsProvider, CommerceProvider, LogisticsProvider, TelephonyProvider.

## Priority integrations
Official WhatsApp Business Platform, Instagram/Facebook messaging, email, SMS, Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, Paystack, Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, generic webhooks/API.

## Rules
- official APIs where available;
- verify webhook signatures;
- idempotent processing;
- integration health and last-sync visibility;
- credentials isolated from frontend code.
