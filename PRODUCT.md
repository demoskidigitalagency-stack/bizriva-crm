# Product

## Name
Bizriva CRM

## Positioning
Commerce CRM and revenue-operations platform for ecommerce, dropshipping and social-commerce businesses.

## Core outcomes
- capture every identifiable customer interaction;
- unify identities and conversations;
- manage leads and follow-ups;
- turn conversations into orders;
- manage products, stock, fulfilment, delivery, COD and remittance;
- reactivate customers;
- connect marketing and ads to delivered-order economics.

## Initial market
Nigeria-first operations with global country, currency and market support.

## Deferred from V1
Full payroll, full double-entry accounting, manufacturing, multi-vendor marketplace, native mobile apps, advanced reseller platform and full ad-creation suite.
